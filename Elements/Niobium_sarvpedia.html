नायोबियम (Niobium)

Atomic Number: 41 · Symbol: Nb

नायोबियम आवर्त सारणी का एक रासायनिक तत्व है जिसका प्रतीक Nb तथा परमाणु क्रमांक 41 है। यह आवर्त सारणी के समूह 5 तथा आवर्त 5 का तत्व है और संक्रमण धातु श्रेणी में आता है।

परिचय

Niobium is a chemical element with the symbol Nb and atomic number 41. It is a soft, gray, ductile metal that is used primarily in steel production and as an alloying agent. Niobium is known for its high melting point and resistance to corrosion.

समस्थानिक (Isotopes)

Niobium has two stable isotopes, 93Nb and 91Nb, with 93Nb being the most abundant. The isotopes of niobium are used in various applications, including nuclear reactors and research.

भौतिक व रासायनिक गुण

Niobium is a soft, malleable metal with a melting point of 2477°C (4491°F) and a boiling point of 4744°C (8560°F). It is known for its excellent conductivity and resistance to oxidation.

इतिहास व खोज

Niobium was discovered in 1801 by Charles Hatchett, who initially named it columbium. The name was later changed to niobium in honor of Niobe, the daughter of Tantalus in Greek mythology.

Discovery

The element was isolated from columbite, a mineral that contains niobium and tantalum.

Modern Applications

Today, niobium is primarily used in the production of high-strength steel alloys, superconducting materials, and in the aerospace industry.

अनुप्रयोग एवं उपयोगिता

Niobium's unique properties make it valuable in various fields:

1. Steel Production

Used as an alloying agent to improve the strength and toughness of steel.

2. Superconductors

Utilized in superconducting materials for applications in MRI machines and particle accelerators.

3. Aerospace

Applied in aerospace components due to its high melting point and resistance to corrosion.

4. Electronics

Used in capacitors and other electronic components.

ब्रह्मांड में उपस्थिति

Niobium is formed in stars during nucleosynthesis and is found in trace amounts in the Earth's crust, primarily in minerals such as columbite and pyrochlore.

उत्पादन व निष्कर्षण

Niobium is primarily extracted from columbite and pyrochlore ores through mining and refining processes. Major producers include Brazil, Canada, and Australia.

सुरक्षा व सावधानियाँ

Niobium is generally considered safe and non-toxic, but safety precautions should be taken during industrial handling to avoid inhalation of dust or fumes.

निष्कर्ष

Niobium's unique properties and versatility have made it an essential material in modern industry, particularly in steel production and superconducting applications.
