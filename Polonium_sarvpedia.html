पोलोनियम (Polonium)

Atomic Number: 84 · Symbol: Po

पोलोनियम आवर्त सारणी का एक रासायनिक तत्व है जिसका प्रतीक Po तथा परमाणु क्रमांक 84 है। यह आवर्त सारणी के समूह 16 तथा आवर्त 6 का तत्व है और संक्रमणोत्तर धातु श्रेणी में आता है।

परिचय

Polonium is a chemical element with the symbol Po and atomic number 84. It is a rare and highly radioactive metalloid that was discovered in 1898 by Marie Curie and her husband Pierre Curie while they were studying radioactivity. Polonium is named after Poland, the homeland of Marie Curie.

Polonium is primarily used in research and as a heat source in space equipment, but its radioactivity poses significant health risks.

समस्थानिक (Isotopes)

Polonium has several isotopes, with 210Po being the most stable and significant, having a half-life of about 138.4 days. Other isotopes include 208Po and 209Po, each with varying half-lives and properties.

भौतिक व रासायनिक गुण

Polonium is a silvery-gray metal that is highly radioactive and toxic. It is a metalloid, exhibiting properties of both metals and non-metals.

Physical Properties

Polonium has a density of 9.2 g/cm³, a melting point of 254°C (489°F), and a boiling point of 962°C (1764°F). Its crystalline structure is hexagonal close-packed.

Chemical Properties

Polonium reacts with oxygen to form polonium dioxide (PoO₂) and can also react with halogens. It exhibits oxidation states of +2 and +4, with the +4 state being the most stable.

इतिहास व खोज

Polonium was discovered in 1898 by Marie Curie and Pierre Curie while they were studying uranium ore. The element was isolated from pitchblende and was named after Poland to honor Marie Curie's homeland. Polonium's radioactivity was a significant contribution to the study of radioactivity and nuclear physics.

अनुप्रयोग एवं उपयोगिता

Polonium's unique properties make it valuable in several applications:

1. Research

Polonium is used in scientific research, particularly in studies related to radioactivity and nuclear physics.

2. Space Equipment

Polonium-210 is used as a heat source in radioisotope thermoelectric generators (RTGs) for space missions.

3. Antistatic Devices

Polonium is used in some antistatic devices to eliminate static electricity in certain applications.

ब्रह्मांड में उपस्थिति

Polonium is produced in trace amounts in nature through the decay of uranium and thorium. Its cosmic abundance is extremely low, and it is not found in significant quantities on Earth.

उत्पादन व निष्कर्षण

Polonium is produced in nuclear reactors through the neutron bombardment of bismuth-209. It can also be extracted from the decay of uranium and thorium ores.

सुरक्षा व सावधानियाँ

Polonium is highly radioactive and poses significant health risks if handled improperly. It is toxic and can cause serious health issues, including cancer, if ingested or inhaled. Strict safety protocols are essential when working with polonium and its compounds.

निष्कर्ष

Polonium's unique properties and applications in research and technology highlight its importance in modern science. However, its radioactivity and toxicity necessitate careful handling and safety measures.
