रदरफोर्डियम (Rutherfordium)

Atomic Number: 104 · Symbol: Rf

रदरफोर्डियम आवर्त सारणी का एक रासायनिक तत्व है जिसका प्रतीक Rf तथा परमाणु क्रमांक 104 है। यह आवर्त सारणी के समूह 4 तथा आवर्त 7 का तत्व है और संक्रमण धातु श्रेणी में आता है।

परिचय

Rutherfodium is a synthetic chemical element with the symbol Rf and atomic number 104. It is a member of the transactinide series and is classified as a transition metal. Rutherfodium was first synthesized in 1964 by a team of scientists at the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna, Russia.

The element is named in honor of Ernest Rutherford, a pioneer in nuclear physics. Due to its short half-life and the difficulty of producing it, rutherfodium has no significant commercial applications and is primarily of interest for scientific research.

As a synthetic element, rutherfodium has not been studied extensively, and its properties are not well characterized. However, it is expected to exhibit properties similar to those of other elements in group 4, particularly zirconium and hafnium.

Rutherfodium is predicted to be a solid at room temperature, with a metallic appearance. Its density and melting point are not well established due to the limited amount of material produced.

Due to its extreme radioactivity and short half-life, rutherfodium has no practical applications outside of scientific research.

As a synthetic element, rutherfodium is not found naturally in the universe. It is produced in laboratories through nuclear reactions and is primarily of interest for research in nuclear physics and chemistry.
